GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity
before you begin.

A learning space that leaves you in control.

Where should I start?

Pick one goal and create a study block. You can also begin with a public guide in the Learning Hub. Add partners, projects or focus sessions when they help.

Are my reflections public?

No. Your reflection text and audio stay account-scoped. Public portfolio sharing is optional, and you can preview what it includes before publishing.

How does weekly coaching work?

Only when you request it, your selected week’s completed reflection text and metrics are sent to Groq to generate suggestions. You can use planning and reflections without requesting coaching.

What happens when I request a tutor?

Choose an available slot and send a request. The tutor confirms or declines it. A pending request is not a confirmed session.
