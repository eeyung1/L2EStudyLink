PLAN · STUDY · REFLECT

A useful week starts
with one doable thing.

You do not need a perfect schedule. Start with a block you can show up for, and use reflection to choose what comes next.

  1. Give your goal some time

    Create personal study blocks with a purpose. Keep tutoring availability separate, so others know when they can request your help.

  2. Find good company

    Discover fellows by skill, request a session, join a focus room or build a project with an agreed team.

  3. Look back, then adjust

    Save a reflection and review your weekly coverage. Optional coaching offers suggestions from your own selected-week records.
